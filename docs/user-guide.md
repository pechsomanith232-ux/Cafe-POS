# Café POS — short user guide

## Cashier

**Start of day.** Open **POS**. Count the drawer and type the opening float in USD and riel, then **Open shift**. You can't sell without an open shift.

**Sell.** Tap a product. If it has sizes or options a window opens: choose, set the quantity, and add it. The cart on the right shows the total in USD and riel. Use **−/+** to change quantities and **Discount** for a line discount. Discounts above your limit ask for a manager PIN.

**Dine-in or takeaway.** Use the toggle above the cart and pick a table for dine-in.

**Hold.** **Hold** parks the bill; find it again under **Orders**. **Kitchen** sends the order to the kitchen screen without paying yet.

**Pay.** **Pay** (F8) → choose the method (Cash, KHQR, Card…) and the currency → tap a quick-cash button, **Exact**, or type an amount and **Add**. Add a second payment for a split (for example $5 cash and the rest in riel). Pick the currency for the change, then **Complete payment**. Print the receipt or start a **New order**.

**Mistakes.** Before payment: open the bill and void it (manager PIN and a reason). After payment: **Menu → Recent orders & refunds → Refund**, choose the items, whether they go back into stock, the reason, and get the manager PIN.

**Cash in/out.** **Menu → Cash in / out** for change top-ups or small purchases from the drawer, always with a reason.

**End of day.** **Menu → Close shift**. Count the cash per currency and type it in. A difference above the limit needs a reason and a manager PIN. Print the Z report.

Shortcuts: **F2** search, **F8** pay, **F9** hold.

## Barista / kitchen

Open **Kitchen**. New orders appear within a few seconds with a sound. Tap a line to move it **pending → preparing → ready → served**. The colour shows waiting time: green under 5 minutes, amber under 10, red after that.

## Stock keeper

- **Receive goods:** Purchasing → Receipts → New. Choose the supplier, enter the invoice number, date and lines in any unit (case, bottle…), add delivery as an extra cost, and post. Stock and average cost update at once.
- **Bought at the market and paid on the spot:** use **Quick purchase** (also in the POS menu) and tick the paid-from-drawer box if the cash came from the till.
- **Waste, damage, staff meals:** Inventory → Adjustments → New, with a reason, then post.
- **Stock count:** Inventory → Counts → New. Enter what is on the shelf and post; the system corrects the differences.
- **Between branches:** Inventory → Transfers. The sender sends, the receiver confirms what arrived.
- **Low stock:** the badge in the sidebar and Reports → Suggested reorder.

Stock history is never edited. A wrong entry is fixed with a new adjustment.

## Manager / owner

- **Menu:** Menu → Products. Each size has a recipe; the screen shows cost and margin. *Deduct stock on sale* can be switched off per product, size or option.
- **Suppliers and what you owe:** Purchasing → Payments to pay invoices (oldest first by default); Reports → AP aging shows what is overdue.
- **Customers on account:** Customers → set a credit limit; cashiers with permission can then choose *Charge to account*.
- **Reports:** sales, profit, stock value, shifts, voids and refunds. Every report has a branch and date filter, CSV export and print.
- **People:** Settings → Users to add staff, give a role per branch and set manager PINs. Settings → Roles to change what a role may do.
- **Audit log:** every void, refund, discount override, stock adjustment, price and role change, with who and when.

## Installing on a tablet

Open the site in Chrome, then **⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app**. It opens full-screen on the POS. The app needs an internet connection: a red bar appears when the tablet is offline, and nothing can be saved until it is back.
